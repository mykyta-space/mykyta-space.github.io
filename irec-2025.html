IREC 2025

7th place in the COTS solid motor category, and 12th overall out of more than 170 teams.

The competition

My second IREC with SEDS RD at ASU. Our team competed in the commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) solid motor category and won 7th place in the category, and 12th place overall out of more than 170 teams from around the world.

My role

As the team grew, I worked as part of a large team with many specialized subteams. My roles across IREC included Propulsion Co-Lead, Lead Machinist, composite manufacturing, and CAD and simulation.
