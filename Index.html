Projects

IREC 2026

Jun 2025 – Jun 2026 · 4th place, SRAD hybrid motor category

My third year at the Intercollegiate Rocket Engineering Competition (IREC), the largest rocketry competition in the world, with SEDS RD at ASU. Our team moved up to the harder category of student researched and developed (SRAD) hybrid motor rockets and won 4th place.

Our rocket flew on the custom M527 hybrid motor that I developed with Michael Bravo, SEDS RD President. Before the competition, we built Cactus Cooler: a 12 ft single-stage fiberglass rocket designed to fly to 7,000 ft at Mach 1.5, as a testbed for the motor performance and our custom avionics.

Tools: Hybrid propulsion, fiberglass composites, custom avionics, Fusion 360, OpenRocket, RASAero

Hybrids Research Capstone

Sep 2025 – Jun 2026 · Custom Hybrid Motor M527

With Michael Bravo, SEDS RD President, I developed a custom liquid nitrous oxide hybrid rocket motor for IREC 2026. The motor uses a 3D printed fuel grain with complex geometry and reached 600.51 lbf peak thrust.

To test the motor, we developed a static fire test stand with a load cell and a fueling setup, and conducted 8 hot fires. I diagnosed and fixed defective valves, leaking piping, cryogenic temperature management, and suboptimal ignition.

Tools: Hybrid propulsion, liquid nitrous oxide, 3D printed fuel grain, load cell data acquisition, static fire test stand, cryogenic fueling
