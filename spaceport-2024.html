Spaceport 2024

My first IREC, with a small restarted team, and the Sportsmanship Award.

The competition

My first IREC, at the Spaceport America Cup, with SEDS RD at ASU. The club restarted in 2024, and I was part of it from the beginning. On a small team, I worked personally on every part of our rocket, Project Wumbo. Our team won the Sportsmanship Award.

L1 and L2 certification

In the same year, I earned my NAR Level 1 and Level 2 high-power rocketry certifications. I designed, simulated, launched, and recovered two personal rockets: one significantly modified kit and one built from scratch.
