SEDS RD at ASU Machining Lead

Manufacturing parts for competition rockets and motor test hardware, and teaching new members.

Manufacturing

As Lead Machinist for the Students for the Exploration and Development of Space Rocketry Division (SEDS RD) at ASU, I manufacture parts for our competition rockets and motor test hardware.

Leadership

I grew from a member of a small team working on every part of the rocket to leading others on complex collaborative projects. I also led a Rocketry Certification Class of 8 new members, where I taught rocketry fundamentals and guided them to their L1 certification.
